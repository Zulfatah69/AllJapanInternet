# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Customers and administrators of the AllJapanInternet platform. Customers are looking to easily purchase internet plans (monthly, yearly, wifi), while administrators use the panel to manage products, promos, and catalogs.

## Product Purpose
To provide a reliable, efficient, and straightforward platform for managing and purchasing internet packages. Success means a seamless workflow for both admins and end-users.

## Brand Personality
Professional, modern, and clean. It evokes trust, reliability, and ease of use.

## Anti-references
No cluttered or overly colorful marketing pages. Avoid generic SaaS templates. The interface should remain focused and functional.

## Design Principles
- **Practice what you preach:** Ensure the interface is as reliable and fast as the internet service being sold.
- **Show, don't tell:** Make data and actions self-evident rather than relying on heavy explanatory text.
- **Expert confidence:** Keep layouts clean and structured to project professionalism.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Standard web accessibility, ensuring clear contrast ratios and proper keyboard navigation support for all interactive elements.
